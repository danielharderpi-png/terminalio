VanishPoint

Add to Chrome Add to Edge
> Automated privacy and tracking guard.

Take back your browsing experience. VanishPoint is a lightweight, zero-configuration privacy extension that works entirely in the background to stop invasive web tracking and eliminate consent popup fatigue.

Instead of fighting with privacy settings on every new website you visit, VanishPoint automates the process.

Shield Features
> Global Privacy Control (GPC) Enforcement: VanishPoint dynamically modifies your browser's network requests to broadcast the Sec-GPC: 1 signal. On legally compliant websites, this legally binds them to opt you out of data sharing and tracking before the page even finishes loading.
> Cookie Banner Auto-Reject: Tired of manually clicking "Reject All"? For sites that ignore GPC signals, VanishPoint hunts down and automatically clicks the opt-out buttons on major Consent Management Platforms (like OneTrust and Cookiebot). You never even see the popup.
> Zero-Breakage Browsing: VanishPoint only targets marketing, behavioral, and cross-site tracking cookies. "Strictly Necessary" cookies are completely ignored, meaning your logins, shopping carts, and site navigation remain fully intact.
> Sleek, Minimalist Dashboard: A clean, dark-themed UI that tracks your active privacy sessions and lets you toggle the shield on or off with a single click.
Privacy First

VanishPoint is built for absolute privacy. It runs 100% locally on your machine. It does not phone home, it does not track your browsing history, and it does not send any of your data to external servers.
